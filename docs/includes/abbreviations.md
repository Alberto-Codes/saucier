*[mother]: A preparation the source itself names as foundational. Escoffier names five.
*[mothers]: Preparations the source itself names as foundational. Escoffier names five.
*[preparation]: One numbered entry that describes something to make. The unit this project extracts.
*[preparations]: Numbered entries that describe something to make. The unit this project extracts.
*[catalogue]: Every preparation read from one source, plus the mothers that source names.
*[concept]: The language-independent identity a term denotes. Two terms in two languages can share one.
*[surface form]: A term exactly as the source writes it. Never translated.
*[folding]: Reducing a surface form to a concept id by stripping diacritics, case, and punctuation.
*[unresolved]: The source states no mother. An absence of evidence, never evidence of absence.
*[derivation]: The relation from a preparation to the mother its opening paragraph names.
*[provenance]: The source, entry number, and line every extracted claim carries.
*[gloss]: An English rendering of a term, recorded beside it and never in place of it.
*[Diataxis]: A documentation framework separating tutorial, how-to, reference, and explanation.
*[ADR]: Architecture Decision Record.
*[STE]: Simplified Technical English, the ASD-STE100 controlled-language standard.
